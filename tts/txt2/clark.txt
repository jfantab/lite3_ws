Hi everyone! Welcome to San José State University! 
Today we’ll be touring Clark Hall, a central hub for student support and academic resources right in the heart of campus. 
Originally the site of the Robert D. Clark Library, Clark Hall now houses a variety of student-focused programs and services, 
including the Study Abroad program, Americorps, the Writing Center, ACCESS Center, International Programs and Services, 
the Peer Mentor Center, and the Educational Opportunity Program (EOP). The building also features classrooms, computer labs, 
group study rooms, and conference spaces, making it a vibrant spot for both learning and collaboration.
Clark Hall is especially important for students in the College of Humanities and the Arts, with departments like Humanities, 
Linguistics, and World Languages located here. The Humanities and Arts Student Success Center (HASSC) provides advising, academic planning, 
and career exploration events to help students succeed throughout their academic journey. Whether you’re looking for academic support, 
a quiet place to study, or opportunities to connect with mentors and peers, Clark Hall is a welcoming space dedicated to helping Spartans thrive.
