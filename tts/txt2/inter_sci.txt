Hi everyone! Welcome to San José State University! 
Today we’ll be taking a look at the Interdisciplinary Science Building, SJSU’s newest and most advanced facility for science education and research. 
Located at the western edge of campus, this eight-story, 164,000-square-foot building is home to the College of Science and select programs from the College of Professional and Global Education. 
The ISB features state-of-the-art teaching and research labs for biology and chemistry, high-performance computing spaces, and the nationally acclaimed Wildfire Interdisciplinary Research Center on the seventh floor, 
making it a hub for innovation and scientific discovery.
What sets the ISB apart is its focus on collaboration and flexibility. Each floor includes “collaboratories” and informal “sticky spaces” where students, faculty, and researchers can connect, share ideas, and work on interdisciplinary projects. 
The building’s open, transparent design encourages interaction and showcases the dynamic research happening inside. Whether you’re interested in biomedical research, data analytics, or tackling global challenges like wildfires, 
the ISB is where Spartans come together to push the boundaries of science and prepare for impactful careers in Silicon Valley and beyond.
