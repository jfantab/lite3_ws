Hi everyone! Welcome to San José State University! Today we’ll be taking a look at the Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Library, or MLK Library, one of the largest and most innovative joint university-public libraries in the country. 
Located in downtown San José, this impressive eight-story building spans over 475,000 square feet and houses more than 1.5 million volumes, serving both SJSU students and the broader city community. 
The library features over 300 public computers, 40 group study rooms, free Wi-Fi, and a variety of collaborative spaces, making it a central hub for studying, research, and campus life.
Inside the MLK Library, you’ll find specialized resources like the Beethoven Center, Steinbeck Center, and California History Room, as well as the Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Civil Rights Collection. 
Unique spaces include the Digital Humanities Center, the KLEVR Virtual Reality Lab, and the Materials Library for hands-on exploration. Students can borrow laptops and equipment, reserve study rooms, and access graduate-only spaces. 
Whether you’re preparing for finals, working on a group project, or exploring rare archives, the MLK Library is a vibrant and welcoming space dedicated to supporting your academic journey at SJSU.
