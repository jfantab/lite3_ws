Hi everyone! Welcome to San José State University! Today we’ll be exploring the Diaz Compean Student Union, the vibrant center of campus life and a favorite gathering spot for Spartans. 
Located in the heart of campus, the Student Union offers a wide range of amenities, including food courts, lounge areas, meeting rooms, and the campus bookstore. 
Whether you’re grabbing a meal, studying between classes, or meeting up with friends, this is the place where students come together to relax, connect, and recharge.
The Student Union also hosts numerous campus services and resources, from the Information Center and Lost and Found to event spaces like the ballroom and various meeting rooms. 
Throughout the year, it’s home to dozens of student events, club activities, and cultural celebrations that make campus life exciting and inclusive.
With its modern design and welcoming atmosphere, the Student Union truly serves as the social and organizational heart of SJSU, supporting students’ academic, social, and personal growth every day.
