Hi everyone! Welcome to San José State University! Today we’ll be taking a look at the Boccardo Business Complex, home to the Lucas College and Graduate School of Business, right here in the heart of Silicon Valley. 
Built in 1971, this building houses the Business Student Advisement Center, where business majors and minors receive academic counseling and support. 
The business program at SJSU is AACSB-accredited and offers a wide range of concentrations, including Accounting, Finance, Marketing, Management Information Systems, Entrepreneurship, and more-giving students the tools they need for success in today’s competitive business world.
Inside the Boccardo Business Complex, you’ll find modern classrooms, a free computer lab on the third floor, and spaces dedicated to student success like the Jack Holland Student Success Center. 
With strong ties to local industry and over 80% of alumni living and working in Silicon Valley, students here benefit from hands-on learning, networking opportunities, and career connections that extend far beyond graduation. 
Whether you're interested in launching a startup, managing global teams, or analyzing financial markets, the Boccardo Business Complex is where future business leaders get their start.
