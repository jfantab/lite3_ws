Hi everyone! Welcome to San José State University! Today we’ll be taking a look at Dudley Moorhead Hall, a key academic building that brings together several departments in the social sciences and humanities.
Originally opened as Centennial Hall to mark SJSU’s 100th anniversary, it was later renamed to honor Professor Dudley Moorhead. 
Here, you’ll find the departments of History, Psychology, Social Science, Economics, Sociology, Women’s Studies, and Asian-American Studies, making it a hub for students interested in understanding society, culture, and human behavior.
Dudley Moorhead Hall is also home to the Instructional Resource Center (IRC), where students can check out video and media equipment for free with their student ID-a great resource for class projects and presentations. 
The building features classrooms, faculty offices, and spaces for academic advising and support. 
Whether you’re exploring the roots of social change, studying economic trends, or preparing for a career in public service, Dudley Moorhead Hall offers a collaborative environment where Spartans gain the knowledge and skills to make an impact in their communities.
