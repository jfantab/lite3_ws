Hi everyone! Welcome to San José State University! Today we’ll be taking a look at MacQuarrie Hall, a key academic building dedicated to President Thomas MacQuarrie, who served SJSU from 1927 to 1952. 
MacQuarrie Hall is home to a diverse set of departments, including Applied Mathematics, Computer Science, Justice Studies, Forensic Science, Hospitality Management, Human Rights, Legal Studies, and Health Science and Recreation. 
Notably, the Administration of Justice program here was the first bachelor’s degree of its kind in the nation, and the Forensic Science department regularly organizes mock crime scenes on the lawn for hands-on student learning.
Inside MacQuarrie Hall, you’ll find modern classrooms, faculty offices, and the Student Success Center, which offers tutoring and academic support. 
Unique among campus buildings, MacQuarrie Hall has no classrooms on the first level-instead, the ground floor features Just Below, a popular deli serving sandwiches, coffee, and snacks, making it a favorite stop for students between classes. 
Whether you’re interested in data science, law, public health, or hospitality, MacQuarrie Hall brings together a wide range of disciplines and resources to help Spartans succeed in their academic and professional journeys.
